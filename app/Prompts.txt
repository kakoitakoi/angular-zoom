CFT (Core Banking System) schema represents a financial transactional system built around clients, accounts, and transactions.

Core entities:
- Clients → own accounts  
- Accounts → store financial activity  
- Transactions (postings) → represent all money movements (debit/credit)  
- Documents → business operations generating transactions  
- Products → define financial logic (loans, deposits, cards)

Key principles:
- Transactions are the single source of truth  
- Balances are derived as SUM of transactions  
- Debit decreases balance, credit increases balance  
- Transactions are immutable (append-only)

Relationships:
- client → account (1:N)  
- account → transaction (1:N)  
- document → transaction (1:N)

Notes for SQL:
- Always calculate metrics from transactions, not balances  
- Use aggregations (SUM, GROUP BY)  
- Join clients, accounts, and transactions for business queries
